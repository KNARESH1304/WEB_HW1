About Me

I am a graduate student pursuing a Master's in Computer Science at Georgia State University with a 4.0 GPA. My work centers on machine learning, cloud systems, and data analysis. Before Georgia State, I completed my Bachelor's in Computer Science and Engineering at SRM University, where I led an IEEE-published research project as a research assistant.
